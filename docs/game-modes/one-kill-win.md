# One Kill Win

A **Game mode** that's over at the first **Capture**. It follows the rules of [Classic](classic.md), with the changes below. Everything else is Classic's: 2 to 4 **Players**, how **Seats** are given out, the random first **Player**, **Safe cells** and protection for stacked **Tokens**, the extra **Roll** after a 6 with no limit, **Leaving** (the last **Player** left wins), and no time limit on a **Turn**.

Terms in bold are defined in [GLOSSARY.md](../../GLOSSARY.md). "Kill" appears in the mode's name only: what wins it is a **Capture**.

## Start

- Each **Seat** starts with 2 of its 4 **Tokens** on its **Start cell**, so the **Game** gets going at once. The other 2 start in its **Yard**, and only a 6 brings one out, as in Classic.

## Moving

- **Tokens** never enter their **Home column**. A **Token** goes round the whole 52-**Cell** **Track** again and again, through its own **Start cell**, and lands on every **Cell** of it, including the one just before its own **Start cell**.
- No **Token** ever reaches **Home**, so there's no exact **Roll** to make, and no extra **Roll** for reaching **Home**.
- A **Seat** always has a **Token** on the **Track**, so every **Roll** has a legal **Move**, and a **Turn** never passes for lack of one.

## Winning

- The first **Player** to **Capture** a **Token** wins, and the **Game** ends at once.
- The winning **Capture** takes every lone opponent's **Token** on the **Cell**, as in Classic. That's usually one, but with 3 or more **Players** it can be two of different **Seats**: when one **Seat**'s lone **Token** shares a **Cell** with another's protected pair, and the pair splits, two lone **Tokens** are left there together.
- There's no limit on **Turns**: a **Game** ends only at a **Capture**, or when every other **Player** **Leaves**.

## On screen

- The **Home columns** and **Home** are greyed out, as no **Token** uses them. This may change.
- The result says who **Captured** whose **Tokens**, naming each **Player** who lost one.
