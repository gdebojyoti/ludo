# Classic

The one **Game mode** in v1. Later Game modes, and the **House rules** a custom mode allows, are described as changes to these rules. The rules that later Game modes are likely to change are marked _(may vary)_.

Terms in bold are defined in [GLOSSARY.md](../../GLOSSARY.md). The rules never depend on colours. Colours are only how each **Player**'s screen draws the **Seats**: their own **Seat** in their own colour and in the same corner, and the others in colours relative to it. Two **Players** can see themselves in the same colour.

## Players and Seats

- A **Game** has 2 to 4 **Players** _(may vary)_, each in their own **Seat**. One **Player** has one **Seat** _(may vary)_.
- The **Host** creates the **Game** and shares its **Game code**. Others join with the code while the **Game** is in the **Lobby**.
- **Seats** are taken in the order **Players** join. The second **Player** sits opposite the first, so a 2-**Player** **Game** uses opposite **Seats**. The third and fourth fill the other two.
- The **Game** starts by itself when the fourth **Player** joins. With 2 or 3 **Players** in, the **Host** can start it sooner. Nobody can join after that, though **Players** already in can **Rejoin**. Once the **Game** starts, the **Host** is an ordinary **Player**.
- There are no teams.

## The Board

- The standard cross-shaped **Board** _(may vary)_: four **Seats**, a **Track** of 52 **Cells**, and a **Home column** of 5 **Cells** for each **Seat**, leading to **Home**.
- Each **Seat** has 4 **Tokens** _(may vary)_, which start in its **Yard**.
- The **Safe cells** are the four **Start cells**, and the four star **Cells**, each 8 **Cells** on from a **Start cell** _(may vary)_.

## Turns

- The server picks the first **Player** at random. Play then goes clockwise around the **Seats**.
- On their **Turn**, a **Player** presses Roll. The server **Rolls** a six-sided die.
- After a **Roll**, the **Player** must **Move** one **Token** if any **Move** is legal, choosing which. If none is legal, the **Turn** passes.
- There's no time limit on a **Turn** _(may vary)_.

## Moving

- Only a 6 brings a **Token** out of the **Yard** onto its **Start cell** _(may vary)_.
- A **Token** moves clockwise around the **Track**, then up its own **Home column** to **Home**.
- A **Token** needs an exact **Roll** to reach **Home**. A **Roll** that would take it past **Home** can't **Move** it.

## Captures

- A **Token** that lands exactly on an opponent's **Token** **Captures** it, sending it back to its **Yard**, unless the opponent's **Token** is on a **Safe cell**.
- Two **Tokens** of the same **Seat** on one **Cell** can't be **Captured** _(may vary)_. Other **Tokens** can still pass them, or land on their **Cell** and share it.
- **Tokens** of different **Seats** can share a **Safe cell**.

## Extra Rolls

A **Player** **Rolls** again, in the same **Turn**, after a **Move** that _(may vary)_:

- used a 6, with no limit on how many 6s in a row
- made a **Capture**
- brought a **Token** **Home**

A **Move** earns one extra **Roll**, even when it does more than one of these. A 6 that can't be **Moved** earns nothing, and the **Turn** passes.

## Leaving

- In the **Lobby**, a **Player** who **Leaves** frees their **Seat**. If the **Host** **Leaves**, the **Game** is cancelled.
- During play, a **Player** who **Leaves** forfeits, and their **Tokens** come off the **Board**. Play continues while 2 or more **Players** remain. If only one remains, they win.

## Winning

The first **Player** to bring all their **Tokens** **Home** wins, and the **Game** ends.
